Privacy Policy for Basse

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Basse (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a baby and toddler tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how your privacy is protected. We designed Basse to keep your family’s data private by default.

Summary: Your child’s tracking data stays on your device and syncs only through your own private iCloud. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can turn off analytics and delete everything at any time.

Who we are

Basse is developed by Hasti, based in Denmark.
Contact: [Basse Support]

Information you enter

You can record information about your child and yourself, including:

  • Child profile: name, sex, and date of birth
  • Sleep, feeding, and diaper entries
  • Milestones, growth measurements (weight, length), and notes
  • Mood check-ins and saved wellbeing notes

This information is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device database. If you have iCloud enabled, it syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple’s private CloudKit database. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account — we cannot access it, and it is never sent to our servers. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Parenting Advice (on-device AI)

On supported devices, the Parenting Advice feature uses Apple Intelligence to generate responses entirely on your device. Your questions and the assistant’s replies are processed locally and are not sent to us or to any third-party server.

Analytics and diagnostics

To understand which features are useful and to fix problems, the app uses:

  • Firebase Analytics (configured without advertising identifiers): anonymous, aggregated usage events such as which screens are opened. This is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash and performance diagnostics to help us fix bugs.

We do not collect the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and the app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. You can turn analytics and crash reporting off at any time in Settings → Privacy → “Share usage data”. These services are provided by Google; their processing is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Basse Plus) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details. We only receive your subscription status (active, expired, in trial) from Apple in order to unlock paid features.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not require an account or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children’s data

Basse is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children. Any information about a child is entered by the adult using the app and is stored privately as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention and deletion

Your entries remain until you delete them. You can permanently remove all data stored on your device at any time in Settings → Delete all my data. To remove synced copies, you can also disable iCloud for the app or delete the app’s data in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. Anonymous analytics/crash data is retained by Google according to their standard retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because your tracked data lives on your own device and iCloud, you are in direct control of it. For any request or question, contact us at [Basse Support].

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at [Basse Support].
