Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you log stays on your device and syncs only through your own private iCloud. We can’t see it, and it’s never sold. See our Privacy Policy for details.

My data isn’t syncing between devices

Basse syncs through iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Basse is enabled in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. A stable internet connection is required, and the first sync can take a few minutes.

What’s free, and what’s in Basse Plus?

Sleep and Feeding tracking are free forever. Basse Plus unlocks Diaper tracking, Milestones, Growth monitoring, Mama Care, and Parenting Advice. New users get a 14-day free trial of everything.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Basse → Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Basse → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Why don’t I see “Parenting Advice”?

Parenting Advice runs privately on your device using Apple Intelligence, so it’s available on supported iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence turned on. On unsupported devices the feature is hidden.

How do I change the app language?

Open Basse → Settings → Language and choose English or Dansk. The change applies immediately.

How do I delete my data?

Open Basse → Settings → Delete all my data to permanently remove everything stored on your device. This can’t be undone.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap the “+” in the corner, search for “Basse”, and add the widget to see today’s feeds, diapers, and sleep at a glance.
